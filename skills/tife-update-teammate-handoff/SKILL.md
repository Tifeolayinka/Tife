# Update Teammate Handoff

Read [workflow](references/workflow.md) and the **Teammate handoff** outline in
[documents](references/documents.md).

1. Identify the recipient, project, and requested task, question, or information. Resolve material
   ambiguity; reuse their handoff document or create one when missing.
2. Add or update items with stable IDs, expected outcomes, context and thread links, blockers, and
   timestamps. Preserve established ownership and conventions; record only supplied commitments.
3. When closing an item, retain its outcome and available evidence in Completed or the project's
   existing archive. Keep open items intact.

**Done when:** each changed item is actionable or clearly informational, its references resolve,
and its status reflects the evidence. Report the document and changed items. Sending messages is
a separate action requiring the user's instruction and a verified destination.
